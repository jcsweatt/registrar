Basic Needs for Students

UCSF Basic Needs for Students supports students with the essential resources to thrive here at UCSF. These resources include:

  • Finance & budget
  • Accessibility
  • Health & wellness
  • Food
  • Housing
  • Transportation
  • Dependent care

Through a robust model of prevention, intervention, and emergency relief, Basic Needs connects students to critical on- and off-campus resources and provides educational opportunities for students to take personal responsibility for their wellness.

Please visit basicneeds.ucsf.edu or send e-mail to [email protected] for more information and to access these resources.
