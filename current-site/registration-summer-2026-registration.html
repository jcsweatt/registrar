Summer 2026 Registration

The Office of the Registrar welcomes students to summer 2026 registration.

Please check the "Summary" tab in the student portal for your course enrollment (study list filing) requirement and deadline. Some deadlines are as early as May 15. Your registration may be cancelled if you do not enroll in courses by the deadline.

Study list filing is open from April 27 to July 31, 2026. You will not be able to enroll in courses if you have a hold on your registration. Please work directly with the office that placed your hold to clear the hold promptly and avoid a late fee for course enrollment.

Fees will be assessed by mid-June.

Some or all instruction for all or part of the academic year may be delivered remotely. Tuition and fees have been set regardless of the method of instruction and will not be refunded in the event instruction occurs remotely for any part of the academic year. Figures for tuition and fees represent currently approved or proposed amounts and may not be final. Actual tuition and fees are subject to change by the University of California as determined to be necessary or appropriate.  Final approved tuition and fee levels may differ from the amounts presented.
