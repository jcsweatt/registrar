First Generation Support Services

Welcome to UCSF!

First Generation Support Services (FGSS) serves the students whose parents or caregivers did not graduate from a four-year college. We are thrilled you’re here, you’re not alone, and you belong. Register with our office!

Approximately 30% of UCSF students identify as First Gen, and we want to support you while you are here. FGSS offers workshops, mentoring opportunities, individual appointments, and community building events. Explore our website to learn about what we have to offer.

Connect with us!

  • Learn More! All incoming first generation students will automatically receive the UCSF First Gen Newsletter that keeps you up to date with community-specific opportunities, events, and stories.
  • Meet us! We’d love to say hello and get to know you and share more details about our services and ways in which we can support you. Please feel free to e-mail us or drop in to see us in the Student Success Center, Millberry Union 100 West on the Parnassus campus.
  • Connect online! Stay connected to our community by joining our Instagram page to keep you up to date with events, student highlights and virtual and engaging resources.
