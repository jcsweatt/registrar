Alumni and Former Students

The University of California, San Francisco (OPE ID: 001319) has authorized the National Student Clearinghouse to act as our agent for all verifications of earned degrees and student enrollment. Employers or authorized third parties should visit www.degreeverify.org or contact the National Student Clearinghouse at 2300 Dulles Station Blvd Ste 220, Herndon VA 20171. Graduates tasked with providing a degree verification should order an official transcript.

The Office of the Registrar may decline to verify degrees of students who have not paid all fees due to the university.

UCSF is a graduate-level health-sciences campus. We offer professional degree programs in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, and nursing, as well as graduate academic programs.

Postdoctoral, Internship & Residency Programs

School of Nursing

Employers:

Please contact The National Student Clearinghouse to verify degrees and certificates. If, after seeking a verification from the National Student Clearinghouse (see above), additional elements of a graduate's record require verification, please email [email protected] for guidance.  

Graduates:

Post Masters graduates prior to Fall 2019: Please email the School of Nursing, at [email protected].

Individuals requiring:
     An out of state (not CA) licensure form completed;
     CGFNS/ NNAS certification requests;
     Confirmation of a specialty (for MS graduates prior to the fall 2013 term);
     Confirmation of MEPN certification;

Please email [email protected] for guidance. For most situations, you will be asked to order a transcript and submit any required forms along with your Transcript Request Form. The Office of the Registrar will forward the transcript and any accompanying forms to the School of Nursing.

UC Berkeley Extension

If the verification or transcript request is for UC Extension courses taken in the 1980s and 1990s, please contact UC Berkeley Extension.

UC Law San Francisco

Please contact UC Law San Francisco for verifications or transcripts from UC Law San Francisco.
