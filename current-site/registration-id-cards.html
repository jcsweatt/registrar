ID Cards

Students must carry their UCSF ID cards at all times while on campus.

Students can obtain an ID card from the Police Department.

For further information, please contact the WeID Program at (415) 476-2088 or visit the WeID Program Web site.

For access to the Millberry Union Fitness Center, please register your ID card with the MU membership office on the 1st floor of Millberry Union East by the central desk.
