Current Students

Students can produce an enrollment verification in the student portal under the “Study List & Grades > Enrollment Verification” section.

Each enrollment verification will include the student's name, program name, degree objective, and admission date. In addition, for every quarter of registration that is selected, the enrollment verification will include the academic calendar date range of that quarter. Students also can choose to include their address and date of birth, and they can select lived or legal name.

Self-service enrollment verifications are free of charge.
