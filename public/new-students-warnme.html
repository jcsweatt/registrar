WarnMe Emergency Notifications

WarnMe is UCSF’s official emergency alert system. WarnMe is activated via text, e-mail, and/or phone when there are impacts to operations or an immediate threat to safety or health affecting the campus community. Please take a moment to add or update your contact numbers of choice in the UCSF Directory.

To ensure you receive WarnMe alerts

  1. Add or update your contact numbers of choice in the UCSF Directory.
  2. Click the “Edit My Record” and log in to MyAccess.
  3. For more information on updating your Directory Contact Record, please visit Directory Services (must be logged in to UCSF MyAccess to view).

For more information, please visit https://beready.ucsf.edu/content/warnme.
