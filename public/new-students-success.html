Student Success Center

The Student Success Center, managed by Student Life, collaborates with campus partners to provide academic, personal, and professional resources for UCSF students.

Location:

Millberry Union West, Room 100

Hours:

In Person

Monday–Friday: 8 a.m.–5 p.m.

Virtual appointments available

Contact:

[email protected] or 415-502-1484

The Success Center brings together a central location of a wide range of student services:

  • Basic Needs for Students
  • First Generation Support Services
  • Guardian Scholars Program
  • Learning Resource Services
  • Registered Campus Organizations
  • Student Disability Services
  • Student Life
  • Student Government
  • Student Rights and Responsibilities
  • Synapse, UCSF student voices
  • Student Veterans and Military Support Services

The Student Success Center helps UCSF students connect to campus partners and orient students successfully to campus and the Bay Area with insider tips, consolidated services, and one-stop information on how things work.
