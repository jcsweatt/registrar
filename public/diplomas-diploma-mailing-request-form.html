Diploma Mailing Request Form

The mailing fee is $20; however, we will mail diplomas or certificates at no charge for School of Nursing students (MEPN, DNP, Supplemental Studies in Advanced Practice in Nursing, Nursing MS, Nursing PhD, and Sociology PhD) who graduated in spring 2006 or later, Master of Translational Medicine students who graduated in spring 2020 or later, School of Pharmacy students who graduated between fall 2006 and fall 2012, and Equity in Brain Health students.

We ship diplomas destined to U.S. addresses by U.S. Postal Service First-Class Mail. A signature will be required upon delivery.

If we mail your diploma and it is returned as undeliverable, the fee to mail it again will be $25.

Spring 2025 and Prior Graduates

Please order your diploma mailing online.

Summer 2025 and Future Graduates

Our office will contact you after your diplomas/certificates are ready for pickup or mailing. In the meantime, please ensure that your mailing address, phone number, and personal email in the Profile section of your Student Portal are up to date. Please read about Student Portal Access for Alumni and Former Students.
