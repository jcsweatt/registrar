Registration & Course Enrollment

Please review our How to Register page for an overview of the registration process. The How to Register page provides the information you need to know for registration each quarter, including paying fees and course enrollment (study list filing).

Statement of Legal Residence

Please submit your Statement of Legal Residence online in the student portal. Your Statement of Legal Residence registration hold will be released after you complete this requirement.

Exception: Students admitted to Advanced Training in Clinical Research, Doctor of Nursing Practice, Nursing Supplemental Advanced Practice - Distance, Nursing Supplemental Advanced Practice, Healthcare Administration and Interprofessional Leadership, International Dentistry, Biomedical Imaging, Post-Baccalaureate programs, Genetic Counselling, and Global Health master's programs do not need to submit a Statement of Legal Residence.
