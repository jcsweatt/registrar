The Office of the Registrar will waive the $15 fee for official transcripts sent to scholarship-granting agencies for students who are scholarship recipients when the scholarship-granting agency requires students to provide a transcript to demonstrate satisfactory academic progress. Students must provide documentation of the scholarship-granting agency's transcript requirement. The Office of the Registrar will not waive the transcript fee for students applying to scholarships.
